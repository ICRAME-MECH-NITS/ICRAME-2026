Review Process & Policy
  • All papers must adhere strictly to the format provided. The maximum page limit is 10 pages.
  • All submissions will undergo a double-blind peer review process.
  • Plagiarism will be strongly checked (max 15% similarity limit allowed overall).
  • The use of content generated by artificial intelligence (AI) in an article (including but not limited to text, figures, images, and code) shall be disclosed in the acknowledgments section of the article.
